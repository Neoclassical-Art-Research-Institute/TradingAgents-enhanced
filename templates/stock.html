Analysis models

Tried top to bottom. If a provider fails mid-analysis (quota, low balance, outage), the next one takes over. Deep runs the research and risk judges; quick runs analysts and debaters.

Web search

Enabled providers are tried in order. A provider that hits a quota or rate limit is skipped for a while (15 min for rate limits, 6 h for quota or auth errors). If every provider fails, analysts carry on without search.
